About John O’Reilly

John O’Reilly is the founder of Wheels on Fire, a website that was made for his years of hands on experience and an undeniable appreciation for finely tuned cars.

He is someone who values perfection, but never takes himself too seriously and brings dry humour into everything he does.

He has built a collection of cars tuned by himself and through this website he wishes to connect with fellow car enthusiasts and create a community. He aims to show his collection of cars and their specs on this website.

He wishes to organise community gatherings and events amongst car enthusiasts.
